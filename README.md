# panchagil.github.io

Personal homepage of Francisca T. Gil-Ureta — https://panchagil.github.io

I'm a Researcher at [CENIA](https://www.cenia.cl/), where I work at the intersection of
Computer Graphics, Vision, and Machine Learning.

Previously, I was a 3D Applied Scientist at [Amazon Visual Innovation Technology](https://www.amazon.science/),
where I worked on 3D reconstruction and 3D modeling. Before Amazon, I completed my Ph.D. in Computer
Graphics at the [Geometric Computing Lab](https://cims.nyu.edu/gcl/) at [New York University](https://www.nyu.edu)
under the supervision of [Denis Zorin](https://cs.nyu.edu/~dzorin/).

I earned my Bachelors degree in Computer Science from [Universidad Catolica de Chile](http://www.puc.cl/),
where I worked with Prof. Miguel Nussbaum. My master thesis was awarded the Roberto Ovalle Aguirre Award
(Best Thesis Project) in 2012, by the Institute of Engineers of Chile.
